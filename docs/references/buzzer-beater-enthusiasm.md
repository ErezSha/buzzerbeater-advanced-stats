# Enthusiasm

For each game (except scrimmages and private leagues which are set to normal by default) you may select an effort level in the lineup page. At the start of the season your enthusiasm will be at 5 and it will always tend back towards 5 throughout the year. The higher your enthusiasm level is the better your players will play. There are three different options that can be used in regards to your team’s enthusiasm. Playing take it easy (TIE) will conserve your players’ energy and have your players play at a lower level of play. The advantage of using TIE is that each time you use it your enthusiasm will increase roughly 33%. For example, if you’re already at 5 enthusiasm and you TIE the next game your enthusiasm will be at 6.65 which will be rounded up to 7. The second enthusiasm option you have is to play normal which means your team will give an average effort. You will not gain or lose any enthusiasm by using this tactic. Finally, playing crunch time (CT) means that your players will play their heart out and do everything they can to win. It comes at a steep price though as your enthusiasm will be cut in half for your next game. It’s only recommended that teams use a crunch time when they are in desperate need for a win. Using a CT all the time will drain your team’s enthusiasm and that will cause them to play poorly even if you continue to CT every game. Don’t forget that throughout the season enthusiasm will always tend back towards 5 and the further it is away from 5 the faster it moves. Lastly, the general belief about enthusiasm is that you can never go into a game with an enthusiasm over 12. It is possible to have a higher enthusiasm than 12 after a completed game but by the time your next game starts to will have dropped.

##  FAQ

### How does enthusiasm work?

Each game (except scrimmages and private leagues which are set to normal by default) you may select an effort level in the lineup page. Next to this there will be a number telling you your enthusiasm level. The higher the enthusiasm level the better your players will play. Playing TIE will mean that your players won’t play as well, but your enthusiasm level will increase by 1/3. Playing Normal will mean that your enthusiasm level will not be affected. Playing CT will mean that your players will play better, but enthusiasm level will be cut in half. However, for all levels of enthusiasm, it will automatically tend back to 5, and the further away from 5 the quicker it jumps back. The enthusiasm is updated at the daily update each day and after each match. The max enthusiasm it is possible to have going into a game is 12.
It is a generally accepted rule that playing TIE with an enthusiasm level of 8 is the same as playing Normal with an enthusiasm level of 5. This is debatable and there are many managers who do not agree with this statement.
